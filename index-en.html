Frequently asked questions

Would you like reassurance before trusting us with your PC ?

Here are answers to the most common questions. For a specific situation, contact us directly.

06 45 76 87 84 →
Do you help both individuals and businesses ?

Yes. We help individuals, freelancers, associations and businesses at home, on their premises, in our workshop or remotely.

Do I need to bring my computer to the workshop ?

Not necessarily. Many problems can be solved at home or remotely. If testing or more extensive repairs are needed, we will offer to take care of it in our workshop.

Are quotes and diagnoses free ?

Quotes are free. A technical diagnosis costs €39 when needed; this amount is fully deducted from the bill if you then ask us to carry out the service.

Can you work remotely and securely ?

Yes. The connection is made only with your permission and you can see everything on your screen. It is closed as soon as the support session ends.

Will my files be kept safe ?

Protecting your data is a priority. We explain any risks before sensitive work and can suggest a backup whenever the situation allows.

Do you also sell computers ?

Yes, new or professionally refurbished. We help you choose a configuration that fits your needs, then prepare and install it.
